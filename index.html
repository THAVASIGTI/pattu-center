About us

Four decades of reading silk by hand.

A pattu saree does not lose its value the day it stops being worn. The silk is still silk, and the zari woven through its border and pallu is still real metal thread. Most families simply have no idea who to take it to.

Sri Kamatchi Pattu Center has been in that trade for over forty years. We buy old silk sarees, silk vetti, shawls and ravikai directly from households across Tamil Nadu — no middlemen, no commission, no waiting for payment. Every piece is handled with the respect a family heirloom deserves.

  • Weighed on a calibrated scale in front of you — never behind a counter.
  • Zari tested by sampling, without damaging the saree.
  • Torn, stained and faded sarees are still bought — condition affects the price, not our acceptance.
  • Cash in hand the same day, or a bank transfer if you prefer it.

Questions

Everything people ask before they sell.

How do you decide what my saree is worth?

Four things: the weight of the silk, its purity, how much zari is woven into the border and pallu, and the overall condition. We weigh each piece on a calibrated scale in front of you and sample the zari without damaging the saree, then explain how those figures produced the price.

Which types of sarees do you buy?

Kanchipuram, Mysore, Banaras, tissue and general pattu sarees, plus silk vetti and angavastram, silk shawls, ravikai and blouse pieces, pattu pavadai, and loose zari or cut borders. If it contains real silk or real zari, we will look at it.

Will you buy torn, stained or damaged sarees?

Yes. Water stains, moth holes and tears reduce the price but never disqualify a saree. The silk and the zari retain value even when the saree can no longer be worn, so please don't throw it away before showing it to us.

Is doorstep pickup really free?

It is free for collections of ten sarees or more, anywhere in Tamil Nadu. There is no travel charge and no obligation to sell — if our price doesn't suit you, we leave and nothing is owed.

Can I exchange my old sarees instead of selling them?

Yes. The full valuation of your old silk can be put towards a new pattu saree rather than taken as cash. Tell us at the time of valuation and we will show you what is available against that amount.

How quickly do I get paid?

Immediately. As soon as you accept the price you are paid in full — cash in hand, UPI or a bank transfer, whichever you prefer. We do not take goods on consignment or pay in instalments.

Can I get a price without visiting the shop?

Send photos on WhatsApp to 90000 00002 — the saree spread out fully, plus a close-up of the border and pallu. We reply with an indicative range. The final figure is confirmed once we have weighed the saree in person.

What is zari melting?

Older sarees often use real silver-gilt zari. Where a border carries enough of it, the thread can be melted and the silver recovered. We handle that process and pay you the metal value, which is usually more than the saree would fetch whole.
